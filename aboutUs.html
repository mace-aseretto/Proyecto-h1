About Us

Behind every handmade piece there is a pair of hands, a tradition, and a story that has been passed down for generations.

TRAMA COLECTIVA is a non-profit initiative that works with Indigenous artisans from Paraguay. We give their craft a place to be discovered, so it can travel beyond the distance and the barriers that have kept it out of reach.

Each piece you find here is a way to support fair income and to help keep a living culture present in the world.
